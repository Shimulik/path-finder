How it works

Tell it how many pieces you want and it finds the mix of pieces that leaves your materials as level as possible

Getting started

  1. Enter your material counts in the BASIC MATS panel. 5.7m, 5,700,000 and 5700K all work, and Paste takes a whole list at once.
  2. Tap the levels you want to craft at, under the amount box.
  3. Enter an amount and hit Find path.

Reading the result

Spread is the standard deviation of what you have left — lower is a more even burn. Range is the gap between your largest and smallest remaining pile. Both are the numbers the solver is actually trying to shrink.

Higher Levels [15 - 50]

For running a single level against whichever sets you like. Pick the level, the rarity, and tick the sets to craft from — search by set name, season, or advanced material.

Sets that need an advanced material work fine here. That material is left out of the optimisation on purpose (there's nothing to trade it off against), but its total consumption is reported with your other materials afterwards.

Loading a code replaces everything you have here, so copy your own first if you want to keep it. Codes stay readable as the game adds gear: sets are stored by name, so a new season simply arrives unselected, and a set that has since been retired is skipped and reported rather than turning into the wrong one.
